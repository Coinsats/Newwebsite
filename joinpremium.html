WHAT IS MSTR BULLS?

One narrative, studied properly, ahead of the cycle.

MSTR Bulls is a group for people who want to know about the potential investment opportunities in Bitcoin treasury companies — MSTR, ASST, Metaplanet and the others building the same model.

These are companies that hold Bitcoin on their balance sheet and finance further purchases through the capital markets. Because of that structure, they do not simply track Bitcoin — they can move at a multiple of it. That is what makes them one of the most interesting opportunities of this cycle, and also why they need to be understood properly rather than bought on a tip.

Inside the group I share exactly what I am investing in, why, at what price, and what my exit plan looks like. The goal is to make +1000% from Bitcoin-related stocks in the next Bitcoin bull market — and to do it with a strategy you understand, not one you are just copying.

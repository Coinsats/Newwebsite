Before we rank · The whole sector

The fee-switch era: DeFi tokens are starting to pay holders

For years most DeFi tokens were "governance only": the protocol made money, the token got nothing. Since late 2025 that has changed. Uniswap switched on its fee burn, Aave made buybacks permanent, and Aerodrome, PancakeSwap, Jupiter and Hyperliquid-style perps send revenue straight to holders. That is the thesis of this video.

1 · Follow the revenue to the tokenProtocol revenue only matters if it reaches holders through burns, buybacks or fee sharing. We check DefiLlama's "holders revenue", not just fees.
2 · Price it like a businessPrice-to-revenue and holder yield tell us what we pay for each dollar. MC/TVL shows how cheap a token is against the money parked in it.
3 · Watch the unlocksA cheap token with 50% of supply still locked is not cheap. Circulating supply and the unlock calendar decide who you are buying from.

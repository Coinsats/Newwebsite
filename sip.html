എന്താണ് Bitcoin SIP?

SIP means Systematic Investment Plan — you invest a fixed amount every month, automatically, no matter what the price is. It is the simplest and most disciplined way for a salaried person to build a Bitcoin position over time.

📅
എല്ലാ മാസവും ഓട്ടോമാറ്റിക്
Set it up once. Every month the money moves from your bank and Bitcoin is bought automatically. No manual work.
⚖️
വില നോക്കി ടെൻഷൻ വേണ്ട
You buy at high prices and low prices alike, so your average cost evens out over the cycle. No need to time the market.
🏛️

3 SIMPLE STEPS

Follow these in order. You only have to do this once.

1
Step 1 : Create Baraka Account
ബറാക്ക ആപ്പ് ഡൗൺലോഡ് ചെയ്ത് KYC പൂർത്തിയാക്കുക

Download the Baraka app and create your account. During sign-up, enter the referral code 6G2DNV so your $30 joining bonus is credited — this is easy to miss, so do not skip it.

Then complete the full KYC verification. You will need to enter your personal details, upload your Emirates ID or passport, and take a selfie for identity verification.

Personal Details → ID Verification → Selfie Verification → Review & Submit

Verification is usually approved within a few hours. You cannot deposit money or invest until KYC is approved, so complete this first.

ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക: Use your own name and details exactly as they appear on your Emirates ID. The bank account you transfer from must also be in your own name, otherwise the deposit will be rejected.
Complete KYC · 4 quick stages
2
Step 2 : Set up monthly recurring transfer on your bank
ബാങ്കിൽ നിന്ന് ബറാക്കയിലേക്ക് മാസംതോറും ഓട്ടോമാറ്റിക് ട്രാൻസ്ഫർ സെറ്റ് ചെയ്യുക

First decide your monthly SIP amount — whatever you can comfortably invest every month without disturbing your regular expenses.

Next, get your Baraka deposit account details from inside the app:

Deposit → US Wallet → Manual Bank Transfer

Copy those bank details. Then open your own mobile or internet banking, add Baraka as a beneficiary, and set up a recurring transfer for your SIP amount.

Set the frequency to Monthly, choose a start date just after your salary date, and set an end date a few years out. Your money will then reach your Baraka US Wallet automatically every month.

ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക: Always keep enough balance in your bank account on the transfer date. If the transfer fails, that month's SIP will not happen. Schedule it 2–3 days after your salary lands.
Your bank app · Recurring → Monthly
3
Step 3 : Set up auto invest on Baraka
ബറാക്കയിൽ Auto-Invest (SIP) സെറ്റ് ചെയ്യുക

This is the step that actually buys your Bitcoin. On the Baraka home screen, tap Auto-Invest, then tap Set up Auto-Invest.

Auto-Invest → Set up Auto-Invest → Select stock

Under "select stock", search for iShares Bitcoin Trust and select IBIT. This is BlackRock's spot Bitcoin ETF — when you buy IBIT, you are getting exposure to real Bitcoin held by the fund.

Now enter your SIP amount in dollars, set the interval to Monthly (or Weekly if you prefer), choose your start date, and tap Confirm.

ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക: Set the Auto-Invest date a few days after your bank transfer date, so the money has already arrived in your US Wallet when Baraka tries to buy.
Home screen · Tap Auto-Invest
Select IBIT · Amount · Monthly · Confirm

ശ്രദ്ധിക്കേണ്ട കാര്യങ്ങൾ

A few things to get right before you start.

ബാങ്കിൽ ബാലൻസ് ഉറപ്പാക്കുക
Keep sufficient funds in your bank account on the transfer date every month. A failed transfer means a missed SIP.
ദീർഘകാലത്തേക്ക് മാത്രം
A Bitcoin SIP is meant for years, not months. Invest only money you will not need in the short term.
വില കുറയുമ്പോൾ നിർത്തരുത്
The whole benefit of a SIP comes from continuing to buy when prices fall. Stopping during a dip defeats the purpose.
Bitcoin വളരെ Volatile ആണ്
Bitcoin can fall 50% or more from its highs. Start only if you are comfortable with that level of volatility.

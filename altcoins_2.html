BlackRock: compute is becoming a new asset class

In "The Machine-Native Economy" (Sep 2026), BlackRock's digital-assets team argues that AI agents will need to find, buy and pay for computing power on their own, and that blockchains are a natural place for this to happen.

"Compute is becoming a distinct, large, and increasingly investable economic resource that could support a new class of digital assets."BlackRock, The Machine-Native Economy, p.7
"Standardized compute contracts could also create claims on compute capacity and related usage rights that can be represented, transferred, pledged as collateral, and settled through programmable infrastructure."p.8
"Agents could query real-time marketplace APIs to compare available capacity by price, performance, latency, location, and hardware specialization, then provision the resources best suited to a given workload."p.9

BlackRock's machine-native compute stack, and the tokens building each layer

Keep it honest: the report names no small-cap tokens. The only assets it mentions are Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and Circle's Arc chain, and it calls agentic payments and compute markets "nascent". BlackRock describes the theme; matching tokens to it is our own analysis.

How this is calculated
  • Altcoin portfolio: $100 bought in each of the 21 tokens at the price on the day it was recommended ($2,100 in total).
  • Benchmarks: on each recommendation day, $100 is split into the benchmark mix at that day's price. ALTvsBTC is 100% Bitcoin; ALTvsMSTR&ASST is 60% MSTR and 40% ASST; ALTvsBTC&Stocks is 50% Bitcoin, 30% MSTR and 20% ASST.
  • Recommendation prices and the BTC, MSTR and ASST prices on each date come from my recommendation log. Live crypto prices come from CoinGecko; live MSTR and ASST prices from Finnhub.
  • Stock picks: $100 in each of the five stocks at the 12 Aug 2026 recommendation price, compared with $100 in Bitcoin at $63,400 the same day. Live stock prices from Finnhub; MPJPY (Metaplanet ADR) trades thinly over the counter, so its last trade can be a few days old.
  • No fees, staking rewards or slippage are included. Recommendations made on 26 Oct have had almost no time to move.
